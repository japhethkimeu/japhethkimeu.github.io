Developed to support the Restore Africa Project, this tool enables partners and stakeholders to identify priority sites for agroforestry, silvopastoral management, and assisted natural regeneration (FMNR) across East and Southern Africa. Integrating land cover, conservation zones, and deforestation trends, it generates maps of priority intervention sites, implementable area estimates, projected carbon mitigation potential, and net present value of implementation costs — providing a foundation for evidence-based restoration planning, stakeholder engagement, and investment decisions.
