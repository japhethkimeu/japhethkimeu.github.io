## About

This is the source repository for my professional portfolio site at [japhethkimeu.github.io](https://japhethkimeu.github.io).

I am a Geospatial Technical Lead with over 10 years of experience applying earth observation, GIS, and AI to conservation, nature-climate solutions, and sustainable agrifood systems across Africa. I currently serve as a Geospatial & AI Technical Lead at FAO and as a GIS & Data Specialist at Connected Conservation Foundation.
